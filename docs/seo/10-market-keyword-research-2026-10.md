# Market keyword research: urban sketching and handmade Chinese brushes

Research date: 18 October 2026. This update supersedes earlier provisional audience/market priorities in this pack. Source IDs M01–M14 are listed below. Read alongside `11-product-seo-and-event-content.md`, `market-keyword-opportunities.csv` and `country-language-research.csv`.

## Confirmed owner brief

The owner confirms urban sketchers as the core audience, frequent urban-sketching events and a substantial photographic archive, short/travel-optimized handmade traditional Chinese brushes, and an additional range for larger paintings. Current sales targets: Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, United States, India and Hong Kong, plus other Asian markets not yet individually prioritized. These are owner-confirmed business facts, not inferred from follower demographics or independently audited sales.

Recommended positioning: **Handmade traditional Chinese brushes for urban sketching and plein-air watercolor, from compact travel tools to expressive large washes.** Professional positioning should be supported by actual workmanship, specifications, demonstrations and artist use rather than a repeated adjective.

“Chinese brushes make more strokes than Western brushes” is the owner's proposed differentiation, not a verified universal comparison. Turn it into original demonstrations of fine lines, belly strokes, washes, broken textures and changes of pressure with named brushes. Compare specific brush forms fairly under the same conditions. Many Western pointed rounds also offer varied marks; do not publish a blanket superiority claim.

## Method and limits

Qualitative search sampling covered English and Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Traditional Chinese seed language, local art retailers, manufacturer product pages, museum instruction and official Urban Sketchers event resources. Queries were run through general web search, not a geo-pinned Google session. Results establish topic/term presence and competing page types, not country rank positions, monthly demand, difficulty, trends or market size.

No Keyword Planner, Search Console, paid keyword database, conversion export, Google Trends comparison or social analytics was available. All CSV volume fields remain blank. “First” means a good relevance/evidence opportunity for this brand, not largest search volume or easiest competition.

Evidence tiers: **A** = exact/near-exact commercial wording on a retrieved primary retailer/manufacturer page; **B** = retrieved related first-party topic/use-case evidence; **C** = brand-fit expansion not directly validated as an exact searched phrase. Native-language candidates require a local artist/editor to check naturalness, ambiguity and purchasing intent before publication.

## Revised buyer hierarchy

1. Core: active urban sketcher attending sketchwalks, painting small-to-medium sketchbooks, choosing portable versatile tools.
2. Core professional segment: experienced plein-air painter, instructor or demonstrator seeking responsive brushes and larger painting capacity.
3. Community multiplier: sketch-group organizer, workshop leader and regional art-store/agent. Needs repeatable product advice, workshop suitability and reliable supply.
4. Adjacent: developing watercolor artist adopting Chinese brush technique; collector/gift buyer follows genuine craft interest.

Do not imply affiliation, official sponsorship or endorsement by the Urban Sketchers organization merely because buyers sketch in urban settings or photographs show a group. Confirm event relationship in each story.
