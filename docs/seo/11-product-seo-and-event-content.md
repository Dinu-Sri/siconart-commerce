## Make every product answer a real artist's choice

Above the fold: approved product name plus a descriptive functional phrase, actual photo, a concise “for whom / what marks / why this model” explanation, true price/stock and exact variant selector. Product names such as Chi Ling remain brand entities; add meaningful descriptors in titles and copy rather than replacing names with keyword strings or changing URLs.

Proposed title patterns, subject to verified suitability:

- `Chi Ling Travel Brush for Urban Sketching | Sicon Art`
- `Feng Short Sketch Brush for Line-and-Wash | Sicon Art`
- `Xiangfei Scroll Chinese Watercolor Brush | Sicon Art`
- `[Approved Model] Large Watercolor Wash Brush | Sicon Art` only after confirming working dimensions and actual coverage evidence.

Do not put all target countries or “best professional handmade premium” into every title. Handmade is a meaningful brand fact confirmed by the owner; per-product materials and construction still need approval. A product page primarily owns a model/attribute transaction; a collection owns the generic category; a blog owns the selection/technique question.

## Suggested product-page sections

| Section | Information and evidence | Search/customer purpose |
|---|---|---|
| At a glance | Intended sketching scale, portability, tip character and verified hair | Immediately distinguishes models with unfamiliar names. |
| What marks it makes | Original labeled thin line, belly stroke, broad wash, dry texture, taper | Explains versatility through visible evidence; no blanket superiority claim. |
| Physical specifications | Tip length, hair-mounting-end diameter, handle/assembled/packed lengths, actual weight | Enables comparison beyond inconsistent size numbers. |
| Short vs travel construction | Fixed short handle or genuine detachable/capped mechanism | Prevents confusion between short brush, collapsible travel brush and reservoir pen. |
| How it behaves | Observed response, loading/release under named conditions and practical limits | Replaces generic claims with useful selection advice. |
| In the sketchbook / on a larger sheet | Actual painting, paper dimensions, artist and exact SKU | Connects brush to intended outcome and demonstrates scale. |
| Seen in a real demonstration | Approved event/article link and caption explaining use | Connects genuine community evidence with the product. |
| Care and packing | Maker-approved cleaning/drying and compatible roll/cap advice | Reduces ownership risk and supports relevant accessories. |
| Delivery and support | Real country-specific availability/quote path, processing vs transit, duties and policies | Converts demand into informed purchases. |
| Compare / next brush | Explain differences from 2–3 genuinely suitable models | Avoids arbitrary related-product grids. |

Keep SKU/variant specifications distinct. A long handle is not evidence of a larger wash, a bigger “size” label is not internationally comparable, and the label Master Grade is not an independent professional certification. Accessories need their own relevant attributes. Do not infer materials from names such as bear, jade, sandalwood or dragon imagery.

## Event archive: editorial evidence, not a gallery dump

The owner confirms substantial event photography. Build an internal asset register before publishing: asset ID, file, original post URL, event title, actual date/place/country, creator, public-use permission, recognizable-person consent where needed, depicted artist, exact product/SKU, painting dimensions, context and factual claim supported. Unknown SKU means the photo cannot prove use of a particular product.

Three useful content forms:

1. **Field guide**: a technique/buying article illustrated with relevant verified event moments. Main keyword is the painting problem; event supplies original evidence.
2. **Event field notes**: an account of a real sketchwalk/demo, including what was painted, tools actually used, artist observations and useful lessons. Event/location intent belongs here.
3. **Artist demonstration**: an attributed painting sequence with brush selection and practical limits. Link exact featured products, with permission.

Potential topic seeds requiring actual matching archive: a Bangkok sketchwalk kit, street-scene line-and-wash in Vietnam, architectural details in Hong Kong, or an Indonesian group demonstration. These are NOT asserted Sicon Art event locations. Publish only events the archive verifies; the country list alone is insufficient.

Avoid repetitive title-swapped city pages, copied social captions, uncaptioned carousels and claims of official USk sponsorship. A past-event article is not automatically an eligible upcoming Event rich result. If actual bookable upcoming workshops are later added, model their real event information separately using then-current guidelines.
