# Buying-intent keyword strategy and commercial blog plan

Updated 18 October 2026 following owner direction: prioritize relevant queries close to a purchase decision, including Chinese brush pricing and comparisons. This file supersedes earlier article ordering in files 07, 10 and 11. Those technique/event topics remain supporting content, not the initial commercial publishing queue.

## Evidence versus opportunity

The companion `buying-intent-keywords.csv` contains 30 commercial query seeds and intended page owners. None has measured monthly search volume, CPC or organic difficulty. Price/comparison intent is promising because it answers a purchase question; it is not proof of high demand. Model-vs-model queries may be small but useful for conversion and internal navigation. Broad queries may bring irrelevant calligraphy, digital brush or low-cost commodity traffic.

Research checked general search results and primary sources: Inkston's Chinese Brush Buyer Guide links material/size choices to its catalog; Jackson's publishes a named-model sable comparison addressing price/value; Palette Exchange lists a Chinese brush series with prices; Joy in Art has bilingual Chinese watercolor/travel-brush offerings. These establish relevant commerce formats and category presence, not volume for an exact Sicon Art keyword, local rankings or Sicon Art's superiority.

## Pricing article contract

State scope explicitly: either **Sicon Art's current retail range** or a properly researched cross-brand sample. A single seller's catalog is not the market-wide Chinese brush price range. Display currency, date/context, variant and in-stock/unpriced status. Use the shared public retail resolver for Sicon Art widgets; never embed private artist/wholesale/MOQ data or stale seed prices.

Compare what readers receive: actual brush dimensions/construction/materials; packing mechanism; standard or genuinely custom handle; real included accessories; verified intended task; support and return terms. Distinguish craft/decoration/edition pricing from demonstrated painting performance. Do not treat a higher price as proof of better marks or counterfeit-proof authenticity.

Country selector or delivery explanation can show supported shipping/quote paths and a transparent subtotal example. Do not call a USD-to-local-currency display a guaranteed checkout price. Do not estimate duties/tax or exchange rates without current verified inputs. Avoid a country price guide unless it adds real local service/checkout information; six title-swapped articles are not a market strategy.

Cross-brand prices require exact model/size, seller URL, checked date, currency, tax/shipping basis and neutral comparison scope. Remove/update discontinued offers; never mix a single brush with a multi-brush set or different sizes as if identical. No need to include named competitor prices until the team can maintain them reliably.

## Comparison article contract

Declare Sicon Art authorship and ambassador/gifted relationships. Record sample model/SKU, measured dimensions, brush condition, paper size/type, paint/dilution, loading method, test sequence and tester. Show fine line, broad mark, water release and relevant textured stroke in consistent conditions, including the limitations. Avoid numeric scores without a meaningful method. Do not extrapolate one sample's results to every Chinese/Western/natural/synthetic brush.

Use a semantic table that includes price basis, working/packed length, actual hair information, observed response, best-fit tasks, limitations and product links. Mark unknowns as unknown. Finish with concrete task-specific recommendations and the exact selected product/variant. Product cards need current prices and honest availability.

Do not produce fake independent reviews, “verified buyer” labels for ambassadors, or head-to-head verdicts when the competitor was never tested. A specs-only comparison can be useful if clearly labelled and sourced; it cannot claim hands-on performance.

## Funnel ownership

- **Buy / price / product name / availability**: product or curated collection owns immediate shopping intent.
- **Price guide / worth it / vs / which / best for task**: commercial blog helps narrow the purchase and links directly to the compared products.
- **Painting technique / events / artist stories**: original evidence and discovery, with contextual links to the commercial guide and real product.
- **Shipping to market / payment / returns**: maintained buying-support information; avoid invented local stock or guaranteed delivered totals.

A price article should not displace product pages from exact model-name searches. A standalone price-list page duplicating shop can cannibalize the shopping path; prefer a useful decision guide plus live products. Care/tutorial pages remain worthwhile but no longer define the first publishing sprint.

## Country keyword validation

For TH/VN/ID/US/IN/HK, expand relevant local seeds with real buying vocabulary (price, compare, buy, worth it, model, size) after native review. Examples for research only: พู่กันจีน ราคา; cọ màu nước giá; kuas cat air harga; 水彩毛筆 價錢. Do not paste these into English metadata or assume literal translations are common searches.

Obtain Keyword Planner data for each market/language and Search Console query/country/page exports when available. Keep raw volumes/ranges and provider/date; separately record intent relevance, actual available products, shipping economics and evidence readiness. Prioritize the intersection, not just the largest volume. Record zero/insufficient-data honestly for niche model names.

## Implementation-plan additions

Add maintained price-comparison/product-recommendation components, exact variant links, query ownership checks, a public pricing-freshness policy, comparison methodology fields, evidence/relationship disclosures, and analytics for product clicks/add-to-cart/purchase from each article. Price changes should invalidate related article widgets and Markdown equivalents. Static narrative thresholds must have validation/update owners so a product cannot remain under-$20 after a price rise.
