## Working rules

- Read the repository's current `AGENTS.md` first. Preserve its English-only storefront, flat English URLs, existing design, retail/private-data boundary, and GHCR/Portainer workflow.
- Recheck the current commit before implementation. Reconcile changes since this baseline.
- All proposed new routes, modules, fields, and components are explicitly proposals. Current paths are identified in the audit.
- Never invent specifications, demand volumes, reviews, delivery promises, legal facts, GTINs, or artist endorsements.
- Start with data accuracy, crawlability, product metadata, and real offers. Optional AI files come later.
- Retain existing product URLs. A search-friendly title does not require changing a slug.
- Work in a `codex/` branch and submit reviewable PRs. Do not deploy simply because this knowledge base was supplied.
- Store this directory under `docs/seo/` in the repository for the implementing agent. Keep it outside `public/`; the internal audit is not storefront content.

## What success means

Visitors and crawlers can discover the products, understand their actual differences, retrieve consistent current prices and availability, and follow original buying and painting guidance. Google rich-result eligibility, rankings, sales, and AI citations remain outcomes to measure, never promises.

The storefront remains English-only. Research now covers six owner-confirmed markets; local-language terms are a proposal, not permission to activate locales. Relative commercial performance and quantified keyword demand remain unknown.
